Scenario

Jonathan and Emil are both part of the same org but have never met. Anton knows both from real ceremonies (⇄ verified).

Try: On Anton's phone → tap Emil → "Introduce…" → pick Jonathan. Jonathan gets Anton's offer; his request goes to Anton, Anton's device forwards it to Emil, Emil decides, and the reply and the vouchers come back the same way. Flip Anton to airplane mode before Jonathan connects — the request waits at the relay until Anton is back, and only then reaches Emil (Jonathan waits until the act expires, 72 hours; without Anton until then, the act fails). Neither side ever sees an anchor Anton holds: both mint fresh cards. Or verify someone yourself: the connect button opens your code and the scanner — including the offline two-way scan.

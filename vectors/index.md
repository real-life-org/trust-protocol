# RLTP Test Vectors

Deterministic test vectors (seal, identity derivation, encounter cards, DTG credentials, visibility, the community anchor's rotation, the group star). Implementations MUST reproduce them byte-for-byte; negatives must fail at their declared stage.

- [`access-anchor-rotate.json`](access-anchor-rotate.json) — `anchor.rotate` under Access 0.56 §5.6 (RLTP-ACC-5950 … 5970): real `rltp-access/0.25` envelopes of a personal community's log founded under a fresh pair anchor — a chain, a first entry with any `prev`, a derivation skip, a rotation onto a new personal community, a `prev` break, an author who is not the sole member (a state-fixture admission), a rotation that does not verify, a repeat (JCS-identical bodies, canonical without further effect), two differing rotations with the same `prev` (the smaller `id` canonical, the other valid but not canonical), a delayed merge with a successor on the losing branch (valid, not canonical), successors on both branches, a successor written after the merge, a nested fork (a second choice under the head that won the first), and two first entries with different `prev` (one the walk never reaches, one it reaches as the winner's successor); per case the status of each `anchor.rotate` (canonical, valid, invalid), the repeats, the canonical chain, its head and the unchanged state. Generated by `scripts/gen-anchor-vectors.mjs` from the library; the oracle is `conformance/access-anchor-rotate.mjs`, re-derived by `conformance/runner.mjs`.
- [`access-conflicts.json`](access-conflicts.json) — authority DAGs under the Access 0.55 conflict matrix (§3.5, §3.6): per case the operations `{ label, id, kind, author, subject, role, preds }`, the expected state (`group`, `forked`, `terminal`), member set, pending exits, retained set, epoch, policy in effect, policy version, newEpoch and retained set at its position per transition, and status per operation (`canonical`, `lapsed`, `removed-disposed`, `forked`, `invalid`), including the fork pairing `policy.change` ∥ enforcement and the fork ended against the fork-free prefix, the lapsing dissolution and its re-issue, one surviving admission against admission-orphaned, transitions of unequal depth and the merged retained set, the resolver whose author a sibling removes, the last-member leave beside a rotation and removals that empty the group, an independent admission in the fork-free prefix, a nested pairing, a resolver concurrent with an enforcement operation, and the resolver's retained set from the reconciled DAG and its emptiness; the field meanings are in the file's `format` member. No schema: the file documents itself. Generated by `scripts/gen-access-conflicts-vector.mjs`, re-derived by `conformance/runner.mjs`.

- [`anchor-rotation.json`](anchor-rotation.json) — `anchor-rotation@1` (Network Visibility 0.30 §6.5), a key-chain link `{ type, prev, next }` signed by both keys: rotations between generations 1–3 of the community anchor of `visibility.json` and onto generation 1 of a new personal community; negatives: a wrong successor signature, a body with a `generation` field, `prev` equal to `next`. Generated by `scripts/gen-anchor-vectors.mjs`.

Generated by `scripts/gen-anchor-vectors.mjs` from the library (entropy stated in the file).
- [`identity-derivation.json`](identity-derivation.json) — includes the community anchor's generations `group/<D>/2`, `/3` (Identity 0.52 §5.4, §6.1) and their rejects (`/1`, `/01`, `/0`, `/9007199254740992`), written by `scripts/gen-anchor-vectors.mjs`.
- [`membership-tasks.json`](membership-tasks.json) — Membership Tasks 0.17 from a real genesis: a `group.genesis` (group DID + founder, `rltp-access/0.25`) whose digest the invite pins, the invitee anchor derived from it, invite, accept and vouch signed under `eddsa-jcs-2022`, the welcome (material `rltp-access-material/0.25` naming the genesis key state, sealed under `rltp/v1/welcome` to the accept card), the admitting `member.add` and its `access-operation/0.1` task, the self-contained re-welcome (`key-delivery/0.1`), and the evidence relay as `membership-evidence/0.1` and `/0.2`. Every case names the `RLTP-MT` rules it proves (`rules`): schema negatives as declared mutations failing at a named point, and coherently re-signed receiver-check negatives failing exactly named checks (evidence pair, vouches, embedded welcome, carrier document, re-welcome). `serviceIdentity` and `keyOpDigest` of the genesis are stand-ins whose derivations the Access vectors own; the runner check that would depend on them is marked `[not-proven]` and proves no rule. Generated by `scripts/gen-membership-tasks-vector.mjs`, re-derived by `conformance/runner.mjs` (`--coverage membership` lists the proven rules).

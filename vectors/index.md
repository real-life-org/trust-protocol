# RLTP Test Vectors

Deterministic test vectors (seal, identity derivation, encounter cards, DTG credentials, visibility). Implementations MUST reproduce them byte-for-byte; negatives must fail at their declared stage.

- [`access-conflicts.json`](access-conflicts.json) — authority DAGs under the Access 0.54 conflict matrix (§3.5, §3.6): per case the operations `{ label, id, kind, author, subject, role, preds }`, the expected state (`group`, `forked`, `terminal`), member set, pending exits, retained set, epoch, policy in effect, policy version, newEpoch and retained set at its position per transition, and status per operation (`canonical`, `lapsed`, `removed-disposed`, `forked`, `invalid`), including the fork pairing `policy.change` ∥ enforcement and the fork ended against the fork-free prefix, the lapsing dissolution and its re-issue, one surviving admission against admission-orphaned, transitions of unequal depth and the merged retained set, the resolver whose author a sibling removes, the last-member leave beside a rotation and removals that empty the group, an independent admission in the fork-free prefix, a nested pairing, a resolver concurrent with an enforcement operation, and the resolver's retained set from the reconciled DAG and its emptiness; the field meanings are in the file's `format` member. No schema: like `member-mapping.json`, the file documents itself. Generated by `scripts/gen-access-conflicts-vector.mjs`, re-derived by `conformance/runner.mjs`.

- [`membership-tasks.json`](membership-tasks.json) — the admission carrier of Membership Tasks 0.17: an `access-operation/0.1` payload (the admitting `member.add`, envelope `rltp-access/0.25`, id and author signature recomputable) with its welcome seal (plaintext `rltp-welcome/0.1`, material `rltp-access-material/0.25` with `keyState`, sealed under HKDF info `rltp/v1/welcome` to the accept card), the task document around it, and the evidence relay as `membership-evidence/0.1` and `/0.2` with vouches; the consent pair, parties and vouch are those of `dtg-credentials.json`. Negatives as declared mutations that fail at a named point (an `rltp-access/0.24` envelope, `rltp-access-material/0.24` or keydist material in the welcome, material without `keyState`, an operation other than `member.add` or with a `transition`, 17 vouches, vouches in `/0.1`) and binding negatives that stay schema-valid (welcome digest, group, subject, accept). Generated by `scripts/gen-membership-tasks-vector.mjs`, re-derived by `conformance/runner.mjs`.

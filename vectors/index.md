# RLTP Test Vectors

Deterministic test vectors (seal, identity derivation, encounter cards, DTG credentials, foreign DTG relationship credentials (informative), the trust layer, the profile, the community anchor's rotation, the group star). Implementations MUST reproduce them byte-for-byte; negatives must fail at their declared stage.

- [`access-anchor-rotate.json`](access-anchor-rotate.json) — `anchor.rotate` under Access 0.57 §5.6 (RLTP-ACC-5950 … 5970): real `rltp-access/0.25` envelopes of a personal community's log founded under a fresh pair anchor, each rotation an `anchor-rotation@2` committing to the generation after its `next` — a chain whose every `next` meets the `commit` of the lineage head, a first entry with any `prev` and a bound chain after it; rejected: a derivation skip, a rotation onto another personal community and one onto a key of another seed (each misses the head's binding); a first entry that misses the binding of the head the walk stands on (valid, not visited), a `prev` break, an author who is not the sole member (a state-fixture admission), a rotation that does not verify, a repeat (JCS-identical bodies, canonical without further effect), two differing rotations with the same `prev` (the smaller `id` canonical, the other valid but not canonical), a delayed merge with a successor on the losing branch (valid, not canonical), successors on both branches, a successor written after the merge, a nested fork (a second choice under the head that won the first, two successors differing in their `commit` alone), and two first entries with different `prev` (one the walk never reaches, one it reaches as the winner's successor); per case the status of each `anchor.rotate` (canonical, valid, invalid), the repeats, the canonical chain, its head and the unchanged state. Generated by `scripts/gen-anchor-vectors.mjs` from the library; the oracle is `conformance/access-anchor-rotate.mjs`, re-derived by `conformance/runner.mjs`.
- [`access-conflicts.json`](access-conflicts.json) — authority DAGs under the Access 0.55 conflict matrix (§3.5, §3.6): per case the operations `{ label, id, kind, author, subject, role, preds }`, the expected state (`group`, `forked`, `terminal`), member set, pending exits, retained set, epoch, policy in effect, policy version, newEpoch and retained set at its position per transition, and status per operation (`canonical`, `lapsed`, `removed-disposed`, `forked`, `invalid`), including the fork pairing `policy.change` ∥ enforcement and the fork ended against the fork-free prefix, the lapsing dissolution and its re-issue, one surviving admission against admission-orphaned, transitions of unequal depth and the merged retained set, the resolver whose author a sibling removes, the last-member leave beside a rotation and removals that empty the group, an independent admission in the fork-free prefix, a nested pairing, a resolver concurrent with an enforcement operation, and the resolver's retained set from the reconciled DAG and its emptiness; the field meanings are in the file's `format` member. No schema: the file documents itself. Generated by `scripts/gen-access-conflicts-vector.mjs`, re-derived by `conformance/runner.mjs`.

The bindings over generations 2–4 of the community anchor of `visibility.json` (multikey bytes, SHA-256, commit), the generation-1 `self-card@2`, rotations 1 → 2 and 2 → 3 with the binding each is checked against; negatives: a wrong successor signature, a body with a `generation` field, `prev` equal to `next`, a `next` that misses the binding held for `prev` (a key of another seed), a missing `commit`.

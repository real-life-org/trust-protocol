Two views of the same protocol. Nothing to install, everything runs in your
tab with real Ed25519, X25519 and AES-GCM, and nothing leaves it.

## The workbench

[Open the workbench](https://web-of-trust.de/simulator/)

The real Web of Trust app on several phones in one trust network. Meet
someone by QR code, verify each other and give trust as a separate,
deliberate act. Watch the network from above, or switch the camera to one
person and see only what they can prove. A clock moves time forward, and the
wire shows every envelope between the phones.

## The ceremony

[Open the ceremony simulator](/simulator/index.html)

One encounter under the microscope: two devices, one delivery channel, the
receive stages of every envelope, clock skew and fault injection, and every
artifact each device stores.
